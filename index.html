What counts?

Classic Tension Board 2 boulders only. Mirror and Spray layouts count equally, as do different board angles. Age is measured in completed years on the ascent date. Climbers of the same record-setting age share the record.

Submissions follow an honor system: no birth certificate or board-angle verification required. Video evidence is encouraged, but not mandatory.

Submit a Record

Think you've set a TB2 age record? Email tb2fossilrecords@gmail.com with your name, age in completed years at ascent, boulder name and V grade, board layout (Spray or Mirror), angle, and ascent date. A link to a video of your send is preferred but not required.

Classic TB2 boulders only. Submissions are reviewed before publication; records follow the honor system.
